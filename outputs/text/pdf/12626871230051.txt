General Information about the School Accountability Report Card (SARC)
SARC Overview By February 1 of each year, every school in California is required by state law to
publish a School Accountability Report Card (SARC). The SARC contains
information about the condition and performance of each California public school.
Under the Local Control Funding Formula (LCFF) all local educational agencies
(LEAs) are required to prepare a Local Control and Accountability Plan (LCAP),
which describes how they intend to meet annual school-specific goals for all pupils,
with specific activities to address state and local priorities. Additionally, data
reported in an LCAP is to be consistent with data reported in the SARC.
- For more information about SARC requirements and access to prior year
reports, see the California Department of Education (CDE) SARC web page at
https://www.cde.ca.gov/ta/ac/sa/.
- For more information about the LCFF or the LCAP, see the CDE LCFF web
page at https://www.cde.ca.gov/fg/aa/lc/.
- For additional information about the school, parents/guardians and community
members should contact the school principal or the district office.
DataQuest DataQuest is an online data tool located on the CDE DataQuest web page at
https://dq.cde.ca.gov/dataquest/ that contains additional information about this
school and comparisons of the school to the district and the county. Specifically,
DataQuest is a dynamic system that provides reports for accountability (e.g., test
data, enrollment, high school graduates, dropouts, course enrollments, staffing,
and data regarding English learners).
California School Dashboard The California School Dashboard (Dashboard)
https://www.caschooldashboard.org/ reflects California’s new accountability and
continuous improvement system and provides information about how LEAs and
schools are meeting the needs of California’s diverse student population. The
Dashboard contains reports that display the performance of LEAs, schools, and
student groups on a set of state and local measures to assist in identifying
strengths, challenges, and areas in need of improvement.
Internet Access Internet access is available at public libraries and other locations that are publicly
accessible (e.g., the California State Library). Access to the Internet at libraries and
public locations is generally provided on a first-come, first-served basis. Other use
restrictions may include the hours of operation, the length of time that a workstation
may be used (depending on availability), the types of software programs available
on a workstation, and the ability to print documents.

2023-24 School Description and Mission Statement
2023-24 School Description and Mission Statement
Mad River High School is an alternative education continuation school located on the McKinleyville High School campus in
McKinleyville, California. The site's goal is to provide an instructional program that includes self-paced academic curricula,
direct instruction classes, social-emotional personal development and responsibility, academic planning, frequent transcript
review, technology based instructional materials, and a focus on career-based transitional activities.
Mad River High School's mission is to serve and support all students through differentiated instruction and trauma-informed,
restorative practices. We strive to encourage and provide opportunities for parent and community involvement, and to ensure a
physically and emotionally safe learning environment in which students can and will thrive.

School Facility Conditions and Planned Improvements
School Facility Conditions and Planned Improvements
During this past decade, McKinleyville High School, on which Mad River High School is located, completed a seven-year
modernization project. One highlight is the renovation of all classroom wings to provide an exemplary learning and teaching
environment. The maintenance and safety of the facilities available to Mad River students and staff is of utmost importance to
the administration of the Northern Humboldt Union High School District. Safety inspections are conducted regularly and safety-
related problems are dealt with promptly and effectively to insure a safe educational environment. The $25.8 Measure Q
general obligation passed by the voters in November of 2010 has and will continue to enhance the campus. Completed
projects include communication and technology upgrades across the campus, renovated bathrooms in the Gym, a remodeled
Library Media Center and new bleachers and a snack bar for the athletic facility, all of which Mad River students may access.
In short, Mad River shares a safe and clean campus with McKinleyville High where the custodial staff and district administration
take great pride in presenting a facility that creates an environment that fosters success.

2022-23 Career Technical Education Programs
2022-23 Career Technical Education Programs
Through the work experience program, Mad River students have the opportunity to seek employment while continuing their
education. Students have the added benefit of securing income while acquiring elective credits toward graduation
requirements. A semester of Technology Essentials is included within the graduation requirements of the school district.
Practical mathematics curriculum builds real-world skills as well as competency with the Common Core Standards. Mad River
students have the opportunity to take Industrial Technology and Art classes through McKinleyville High School. Students in
Special Education receive services via the Workability, WIOA, and TPP programs, which are all administered in-house. In
addition, Mad River administration strives to pair students with community business partners for internships to foster interest in
future employment opportunities.--------

2023-24 Opportunities for Parental Involvement
2023-24 Opportunities for Parental Involvement
Mad River High School has an open-door policy for parents/community/business partners to come and observe, participate in
stakeholder meetings, ask questions, and volunteer. Parents may also take advantage of classes offered by "The Parent

2023-24 School Safety Plan
2023-24 School Safety Plan
Mad River High School takes pride in maintaining a safe physical and emotional environment for its students, staff and the
extended community. As part of the McKinleyville High campus, we have a close working relationship with the Humboldt
County Sheriff's Department, the Arcata Police and Fire Departments and the Humboldt County Office of Education. Mad River
works within a district team to promote professional development for its staff, such as Crisis Prevention Institute training and a
yearly Suspected Child Abuse Reporting training. Each year Mad River students participate in the McKinleyville High School's
emergency drills in order to provide them with the opportunity to practice all of the district's emergency protocols, including
lockdown, earthquake, and fire drills. As part of the district's Safety Committee, Mad River's principal is an active participant in
keeping the district's Safety Plan up to date. The plan is located on the McKinleyville High School website and is reviewed and
updated each school year.--------
