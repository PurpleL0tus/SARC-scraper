The school continues to develop its Career Technical Education (CTE) program. The school recognizes the need to integrate core
academic knowledge with technical skills as needed for today’s modern workforce, and the school is committed to bringing students
greater opportunities in developing these skills through their continued efforts in developing relationships with local businesses and
community colleges. The school offers several professional skills and CTE courses that include: Child, Family & Society, Small Business
Management, Child Development, Introduction to Business, and Medical Terminology.

Chronic Absenteeism
The current Chronic Absentee rate is not calculated in a manner for non-classroom based DASS programs to reflect the nature of
the students served. According to the CDE, students are determined to be chronically absent if they were eligible to be considered
chronically absent at the selected level during the academic year and they were absent for 10% or more of the days they were
expected to attend. Crescent View West follows all of the required CA State School Dashboard requirements.

School Safety School Year 2023-2024
Our school Safety Plan consists of our Injury and Illness Prevention Program, Emergency Procedures and Section 4 of our Employee
handbook. Sponsoring School Districts ask for these safety programs as part of our charter’s renewal process. We review our School
Safety Plans annually and update them if necessary.The safety of students and staff is a primary concern for the school. Both teachers
and administrators supervise the students throughout the school day to ensure a safe and positive learning environment. Both staff
and student’s school IDs are expected to be displayed at all times. Visitors to the sites must register at the front office to receive
appropriate identification badges. The school safety plan was last updated and reviewed with school staff in November 2023.
