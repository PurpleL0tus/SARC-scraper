School Facility Conditions and Planned Improvements School Year 2023-2024
Vista Real Charter High School currently operates six resource centers in Ventura County. The original center is located in Oxnard and
there are five satellite centers in Camarillo, Santa Paula, Simi Valley, Port Hueneme, and Ventura. All facilities are in safe and functioning
condition. All sites meet city and state codes. Vista Real has recently updated the interior design and furniture of all sites to better
meet students’ learning needs. This includes expanding small group instruction spaces and introducing modular furniture to enhance
learning experiences.

Career Technical Education (CTE) Programs School Year 2022-2023
Vista Real recognizes the need to integrate core academic knowledge with technical skills in order to be successful in today’s
modern workforce and continues to develop its Career Technical Education (CTE) program. The school is committed to bringing
students greater opportunities in developing these skills through continued efforts to expand relationships with local businesses,
trade schools, and community colleges. Partnerships with local business, such as the Marriott Hotel partnership, provide real-
world job seeking experience, while CTE courses such as Integrated Health and Patient Care help students explore different
career pathways. The school is currently in the early stages of implementing a new platform called Edymanics where students can
take various career courses that can lead to certification. Additionally, Vista Real has have specific curriculum centered around
development of career skills such as Intro to Computers & Info Tech and Professional skills.
Vista Real also partners with the Ventura County Office of Education and the Ventura County Community College District to give
students access to a variety of career education programs such as medical field training, automotive repair, manufacturing, welding,
and more.

School Safety School Year 2023-2024
The school Safety Plan consists of the Injury and Illness Prevention Program, Emergency Procedures, and Section 4 of the Employee
handbook. Sponsoring School Districts ask for these safety programs as part of the charter’s renewal process. The Safety Plans are
reviewed annually and update, if necessary our School Safety Plans annually and update them, if necessary. The safety of students
and staff is a primary concern for the school. Both teachers and administrators supervise the students throughout the school day to
ensure a safe and positive learning environment. Both staff and student school IDs are expected to be displayed at all times, and
visitors to the sites must register at the front office to receive appropriate identification badges before entering the classroom. The
school safety plan was last updated and reviewed with school staff in November 2023.
