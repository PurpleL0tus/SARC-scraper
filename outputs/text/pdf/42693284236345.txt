The Santa Ynez Valley Union High School District is proud of its fine tradition of
Professional Staff
excellence in secondary education. Educational programs offered by the district
District Expenditures
include a comprehensive high school and a continuation high school. All programs

ensuring the academic success of all students and leaving no child behind by
offering a variety of programs to meet students’ individual needs, such as advanced
placement classes, vocational education, English language development, and
special education.
Santa Ynez Valley Union High School serves students in grades nine through twelve
and follows a traditional calendar. At the beginning of the 2022-23 school year, 847
students were enrolled, including 0.2% foster youth, 0.5% homeless youth, 11.8%
in special education, 3.9% English learners (students who either are in the process
of learning English or who have recently become proficient in English) and 30.1% in
the free and reduced lunch program.
