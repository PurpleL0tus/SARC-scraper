General Information about the School Accountability Report Card (SARC)
SARC Overview By February 1 of each year, every school in California is required by state law to
publish a School Accountability Report Card (SARC). The SARC contains
information about the condition and performance of each California public school.
Under the Local Control Funding Formula (LCFF) all local educational agencies
(LEAs) are required to prepare a Local Control and Accountability Plan (LCAP),
which describes how they intend to meet annual school-specific goals for all pupils,
with specific activities to address state and local priorities. Additionally, data
reported in an LCAP is to be consistent with data reported in the SARC.
- For more information about SARC requirements and access to prior year
reports, see the California Department of Education (CDE) SARC web page at
https://www.cde.ca.gov/ta/ac/sa/.
- For more information about the LCFF or the LCAP, see the CDE LCFF web
page at https://www.cde.ca.gov/fg/aa/lc/.
- For additional information about the school, parents/guardians and community
members should contact the school principal or the district office.
DataQuest DataQuest is an online data tool located on the CDE DataQuest web page at
https://dq.cde.ca.gov/dataquest/ that contains additional information about this
school and comparisons of the school to the district and the county. Specifically,
DataQuest is a dynamic system that provides reports for accountability (e.g., test
data, enrollment, high school graduates, dropouts, course enrollments, staffing,
and data regarding English learners).
California School Dashboard The California School Dashboard (Dashboard)
https://www.caschooldashboard.org/ reflects California’s new accountability and
continuous improvement system and provides information about how LEAs and
schools are meeting the needs of California’s diverse student population. The
Dashboard contains reports that display the performance of LEAs, schools, and
student groups on a set of state and local measures to assist in identifying
strengths, challenges, and areas in need of improvement.
Internet Access Internet access is available at public libraries and other locations that are publicly
accessible (e.g., the California State Library). Access to the Internet at libraries and
public locations is generally provided on a first-come, first-served basis. Other use
restrictions may include the hours of operation, the length of time that a workstation
may be used (depending on availability), the types of software programs available
on a workstation, and the ability to print documents.

2023-24 School Description and Mission Statement
2023-24 School Description and Mission Statement
Welcome to Highland Academy Charter School. Situated in the heart of the San Gorgonio Pass in Beaumont, California,
Highland Academy stands as a locally-funded, independent charter school dedicated to fostering educational excellence for
students in Transitional Kindergarten through 8th grade.
Since its inception in 2013, Highland Academy has been a collaborative endeavor, involving the active participation of local
educators, administrators, parents, and community members. Our primary objective is to facilitate an environment wherein
every student can realize their full potential and discover their innate capabilities, irrespective of the form those may take.
Highland Academy places a strong emphasis on academic achievement, encouraging students to pursue excellence through
the cultivation of qualities such as diligence, self-discipline, and collaborative engagement with peers. Operating within the
educational standards of California, the school provides a project-based learning atmosphere that allows students to explore
educational opportunities.
Our institution adopts a flexible instructional approach, affording educators the latitude to tailor their teaching methods and
leverage diverse resources and interventions to support student learning. At Highland Academy, we are deeply invested in the
individual success of each student, considering their aspirations as our own and committing ourselves to aiding them in
attaining their goals.

School Facility Conditions and Planned Improvements
School Facility Conditions and Planned Improvements
Highland Academy presently leases its campus situated at 715 Wellwood Ave. from the Beaumont Unified School District. The
school assumes responsibility for and ensures the maintenance of the campus, upholding standards of cleanliness, safety, and
functionality. The campus encompasses 17 classrooms, a staff lounge equipped with a copy room, an administrative office, and
a lunchroom. Custodial services are executed on a daily basis, encompassing the clean up and disinfection of all frequently
used and touched surfaces/areas on campus. Highland Academy is committed to ensuring that requisite repairs essential for
the upkeep and operational efficiency of the school are expeditiously addressed. Routine inspections are conducted by
personnel from the school, city, district, and county.
In our capacity as community members, we take great pride in preserving the condition and visual appeal of our school
environment. Over the years, Highland Academy has undertaken numerous enhancements to the facility, including painting,
the installation of sidewalks and curbing, cleaning and repairing exterior windows, reseeding the lawn, implementing energy-
efficient lighting in all campus buildings, and upgrading the Wi-Fi network and emergency response systems. The district has
collaborated in initiatives such as repaving the blacktop across the campus, replacing roofing on multiple buildings to preclude
leaks and water damage, and upgrading/replacing various HVAC units as needed. Additionally, the acquisition and placement
of new exterior tables and benches throughout the campus create conducive areas for students to engage in outdoor study and
lunch activities.

2023-24 Opportunities for Parental Involvement
2023-24 Opportunities for Parental Involvement
Highland Academy Charter School firmly believes in the collaborative pursuit of educational objectives, recognizing the
indispensable role played by parents and family members in this endeavor. Consequently, we actively promote and extend
invitations to parents and family members to become integral participants in their student's educational journey. This approach
is manifested through various avenues, encompassing attendance and support at evening and weekend events, involvement in
our sports and ASB programs, engagement in school-coordinated service opportunities, volunteering within their student's
classroom, and participation in monthly school board meetings.
To facilitate effective communication, our school utilizes Bloomz, ensuring comprehensive outreach to 100% of our families
through email, text, and phone channels as required. We strongly encourage families to initiate contact with both teachers and
administrators when questions or concerns arise, providing multiple avenues for communication. This open-door policy fosters
an unrestricted exchange of information, enabling us to address student needs promptly and effectively.

2023-24 School Safety Plan
2023-24 School Safety Plan
Our school has established a safety committee comprising administrators, educators, and parent volunteers possessing
expertise in the realm of school safety. This committee is tasked with the ongoing enhancement of the safety protocols at the
site, focusing on necessary improvements in the spheres of general safety and disaster preparedness. Highland Academy
conducts routine emergency drills aimed at practicing evacuations and disaster response procedures.
Highland Academy maintains a comprehensive training program, ensuring that all staff members receive instruction in critical
areas such as fire evacuation, first-aid, allergy management, intruder response, suspected child abuse reporting, and anti-
bullying measures. The institution is equipped with twenty-four-hour video surveillance and direct police and fire response
systems to bolster security measures.
In adherence to a proactive safety approach, our school's safety plan undergoes an annual review and update, with the most
recent revision presented to the Board on August 25, 2023. This commitment to regular evaluation and enhancement
underscores our dedication to maintaining a secure and resilient educational environment.

2022-23 Types of Services Funded
Fiscal Year 2022-23 Types of Services Funded
The programs and supplementary services we offer are in accordance with the priorities and objectives delineated in our
school's Local Control Accountability Plan (LCAP). Academic interventions are extended in the subjects of English Language
Arts (ELA) and mathematics for students requiring more intensive academic support. The progress of all students is
systematically monitored and assessed through the utilization of Curriculum Associate's iReady diagnostic program.
Beyond regular school hours, tutoring sessions are made available during both after-school and during lunch periods.
Moreover, we extend after-school child care services until 6:00 pm through our HOWL After School Program.
In regards to student well-being, two dedicated school administrators, academic coach, campus supervisor, and school
counselor are employed to keep students safe and provide guidance and support to students who need assistance in
navigating challenging situations, both within and outside the school environment.

Professional Development
Professional Development
Fridays at Highland Academy are designated as "Early Release Days," providing our staff with weekly occasions for
collaborative endeavors, lesson planning, and participation in staff meetings. Throughout the academic year, teachers receive
sustained support through classroom observations, coaching sessions, and access to external training and conference
opportunities as they arise.
In addition to ongoing support, teachers are allocated two professional development days. During these days, teachers have
the option to secure a substitute to facilitate classroom coverage while they engage in observational activities of their peers on
campus. These days afford teachers the opportunity to collaborate with our school's academic coach, fostering continuous
professional growth and development.
