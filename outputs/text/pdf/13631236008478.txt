Campus Supervision
School administration and teaching staff place
a high priority on providing adequate adult
supervision on campus before, during, and after
school. As students arrive on campus each
morning, the custodian, cafeteria staff, and
student supervisors are strategically assigned
to designated entrance areas, the breakfast
area and the playground. Breakfast is served
every day in the classroom from 8-8:10 a.m.
During recess, teachers supervise playground
activity. The principal and student supervisors
monitor lunch time activity in the cafeteria and
on the playground. At the end of the day when
students are dismissed, the principal, teachers,
and student supervisors monitor student
behavior to ensure a safe and orderly departure.
