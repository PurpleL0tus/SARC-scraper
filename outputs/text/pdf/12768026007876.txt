General Information about the School Accountability Report Card (SARC)
SARC Overview By February 1 of each year, every school in California is required by state law to
publish a School Accountability Report Card (SARC). The SARC contains
information about the condition and performance of each California public school.
Under the Local Control Funding Formula (LCFF) all local educational agencies
(LEAs) are required to prepare a Local Control and Accountability Plan (LCAP),
which describes how they intend to meet annual school-specific goals for all pupils,
with specific activities to address state and local priorities. Additionally, data
reported in an LCAP is to be consistent with data reported in the SARC.
- For more information about SARC requirements and access to prior year
reports, see the California Department of Education (CDE) SARC web page at
https://www.cde.ca.gov/ta/ac/sa/.
- For more information about the LCFF or the LCAP, see the CDE LCFF web
page at https://www.cde.ca.gov/fg/aa/lc/.
- For additional information about the school, parents/guardians and community
members should contact the school principal or the district office.
DataQuest DataQuest is an online data tool located on the CDE DataQuest web page at
https://dq.cde.ca.gov/dataquest/ that contains additional information about this
school and comparisons of the school to the district and the county. Specifically,
DataQuest is a dynamic system that provides reports for accountability (e.g., test
data, enrollment, high school graduates, dropouts, course enrollments, staffing,
and data regarding English learners).

2023-24 School Description and Mission Statement
2023-24 School Description and Mission Statement
Our Vision
Students at Walker Elementary will grow into empathetic, collaborative, and global-minded individuals who embrace diversity
and persevere through adverse situations. They will display positive character traits such as reliability, kindness, honesty, and
integrity, and they will go on to positively influence others while maintaining a passion for learning and thinking critically.
Our Mission
The Walker Elementary School staff is dedicated to the social and emotional welfare, and academic success of all students in a
safe, equitable, and caring environment. Through the promotion of a growth mindset, an engaging and challenging curriculum,
and a supportive school culture, students will gain exceptional skills in all subject areas. We aim to achieve this through
exceptional classroom instruction that utilizes research-based materials, programs, and strategies. Participation in the
educational process by family and community plays an integral role in student success, and we welcome you to join us on that
journey!
School Description:
Walker Elementary School is a Transitional Kindergarten (TK) through Grade 4 school in the community of Fortuna. Our

School Facility Conditions and Planned Improvements
School Facility Conditions and Planned Improvements
Walker Elementary School provides a safe and clean environment for its students. One full-time custodian and two half-time
custodians maintain our facilities. Our school has recently undergone modernization to the exterior of the school using bond
funds. Breezeways, canopies, electrical, plumbing, and internet connectivity have all been updated. Reskinning of existing
classroom wings and some interior upgrades have also taken place. There have also been updates to the handicap-accessible
restrooms, railings for handicap-accessible rampways, energy-efficient lighting in the hallways, and carpeted flooring. The
school has also been re-keyed, and new signage, a clock/communication system, and security cameras are being installed.
Kindergarten through 4th-grade classrooms have Promethean Boards. Additionally, Chromebooks have been purchased to
enhance instruction in grades K-4. TK has iPads. Seven modular buildings have been added to support increased enrollment
and additional programs. A solar array was installed on the school site during the 2021-2022 school year.
During the spring and summer of 2020-2021, the Safe Routes to School Project was used to redo the entrance and exit to both
of the school parking lots. Additionally, the parking lots were reconfigured to add a drop-off lane and a drive-through lane.
Sidewalks were added to several neighboring streets. This project increased safety for walking and biking to school, along with
safer drop-off and pick-up by car.

2023-24 Opportunities for Parental Involvement
2023-24 Opportunities for Parental Involvement
There is a strong partnership between the school and the community. Walker Elementary School prides itself on providing
parents with opportunities to become involved in their child's education. Parents are encouraged to work in the classroom, go
on field trips, and become involved in the School Site Council, PTO, or the ELL Parent Advisory Council. Parents who are
interested in becoming members or attending meetings may contact the principal through the school office for dates and times
of the meetings, or refer to the school's webpage or social media outlets for additional information. The school office can
provide the names and phone numbers of committee officers to anyone who wishes to become involved. In addition, families
are invited to eat lunch with their children at Lunch in the Courtyard events. Families are also invited to EL Family Nights,
concerts, the school carnival, Open House, and other events throughout the year.

2023-24 School Safety Plan
2023-24 School Safety Plan
Walker Elementary School has a comprehensive safety plan that is evaluated annually by the staff and School Site Council to
ensure the safety of our students. This plan covers emergencies such as fires, earthquakes, and disasters and also includes
policies regarding visitors on campus. The most recent disaster drill was carried out on November 2, 2023. Staff and students
practice monthly fire drills and earthquake procedures, as well as schoolwide lockdown procedures. Special attention is also
given to helping all students feel safe. The Comprehensive School Safety Plan was last updated on January 11th, 2023,
reviewed on January 30th, 2023, and approved on February 9th, 2023.

Professional Development
Professional Development
All classified and certificated staff members are provided mandated reporter training, sexual harassment prevention,
homelessness awareness and understanding, bloodborne pathogen exposure prevention, IPM training, and suicide prevention
training. All staff receive ongoing training on positive behavior intervention to ensure the successful implementation of school-
wide PBIS practices. Throughout the school year, early-release Fridays are used for additional training for all staff. The
Humboldt County Office of Education is also utilized for local training and staff development opportunities. The School Site
Council designates Title I funds each year to be used by teachers and staff for additional training to improve student
achievement. Throughout the school year, teachers are given opportunities to choose relevant professional development
opportunities to enhance and improve their classroom instruction techniques and practices.-------
