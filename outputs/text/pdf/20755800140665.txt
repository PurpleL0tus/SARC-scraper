General Information about the School Accountability Report Card (SARC)
SARC Overview By February 1 of each year, every school in California is required by state law to
publish a School Accountability Report Card (SARC). The SARC contains
information about the condition and performance of each California public school.
Under the Local Control Funding Formula (LCFF) all local educational agencies
(LEAs) are required to prepare a Local Control and Accountability Plan (LCAP),
which describes how they intend to meet annual school-specific goals for all pupils,
with specific activities to address state and local priorities. Additionally, data
reported in an LCAP is to be consistent with data reported in the SARC.
- For more information about SARC requirements and access to prior year
reports, see the California Department of Education (CDE) SARC web page at
https://www.cde.ca.gov/ta/ac/sa/.
- For more information about the LCFF or the LCAP, see the CDE LCFF web
page at https://www.cde.ca.gov/fg/aa/lc/.
- For additional information about the school, parents/guardians and community
members should contact the school principal or the district office.

2023-24 School Description and Mission Statement
2023-24 School Description and Mission Statement
Stone Creek Elementary School opened its doors in the fall of 2021 serving 300 students in the residential Riverstone
community located six miles north of Fresno. Two short years later, Stone Creek has an enrollment of 540 students. During
Stone Creek's inaugural year, Stone Creek was awarded the California Distinguished School Award for 2022. Stone Creek is
the third elementary school in Golden Valley Unified School District, which was established in 1998 and is led by a five-member
school board. Stone Creek's students feed into Ranchos Middle School and Liberty Elementary School.

2023-24 School Description and Mission Statement
Stone Creek is committed to "Shaping Hearts and Minds," and the school's behavioral expectations follow the acronym
SHAPE: Show respect, Have integrity, Actively participate, Practice Perseverance, and Express Gratitude. Character lessons
are taught in classrooms as well as reinforced with videos, songs, and activities, such as the Great Kindness Challenge. Stone
Creek's academics are data driven, and the teaching staff follow the initiative of the district to create an effective Professional
Learning Communty, where teachers analyze data, discuss student learning needs, and implement effective strategies. Stone
Creek's academics continue to remain strong with 65% of students meeting or exceeding standards in ELA and 60% meeting
or exceeding standards in math on CAASPP. Stone Creek's PLC will continue to strive for future growth.
Mission:
Stone Creek will inspire our students to develop strong character and achieve continuous academic growth through rigorous
instruction in a safe, caring environment.
Vision:
We will work together as a professional learning community to SHAPE hears and mind through character eduction and rigorous
academics.
Collective Commitments:
*We will model and develop positive, intentional relationships within our Sunbird community.
*We will engage in collaborative learning communitites to develop best teaching practices and support stuent learning.
*We will continuously monitor the academic growth of all students to provide an equitable, rigorous educaiton.

2023-24 Opportunities for Parental Involvement
2023-24 Opportunities for Parental Involvement
Stone Creek Elementary School is very fortunate to enjoy the benefits of supportive, involved parents who show strong support
for the school. The Stone Creek Parent Club meets monthly with a focus on providing classroom supplies and resources over
and above what the District provides, so there can be a richer academic and extra-curricular enviornment. Stone Creek's
School Site Council meets quarterly to advise on the School Site Comprehensive Plan. The Safety committee also includes
parent invovlement as the Comprehensive School Safety Plan is reviewed and updated each year. Stone Creek participates in
the District English Language Advisory Committee with parent representation at the DELAC meetings. Parents also have the
opportunity to partner with Stone Creek through volunteering in classroom, helping teachers with projects, attending sporting
events and field trips, and serving on school and district committees.--------

2023-24 School Safety Plan
2023-24 School Safety Plan
Stone Creek Elementary School has a Comprehensive School Safety Plan that is reviewed and updated annually. Plans are
reviewed with staff annually at the beginning of each school year. Stone Creek teachers met in September for training. Our
Stone Creek School Site Council approves our Comprehensive School Safety Plan after the safety committee has met to
review the plan. Emergency drills are conducted to prepare students and staff for an emergency. Stone Creek strives to
maintian a safe, clean, respectful, and orderly school campus that encourages positive attitudes in students and staff. A joint
effort between students and staff help keep the campus clean and litter free. Stone Creek's custodial staff surveys the campus
as the first order of business each day. They insure the campus is clean, and any vandalism is corrected, and that the campus
is presentable and representative of the priority the Golden Valley Unified School Board sets on safety and appearance. Stone
Creek's Comprehensive School Safety Plan was reviewed and updated on October 4, 2023. The plan with any changes is
approved by the Golden Valley Unified School District Board of Trustees annually.--------

Professional Development
Professional Development
For the first two and a half years of Stone Creek's existence, the Stone Creek staff has participated in Professional Learning
Communities to devleop effective pedgogical practice. Staff development occurs during teacher preparation days and early
release Wednesdays. Stone Creek is following the emphasis on Professional Learning Communities developed by the district
leadership. Golden Valley Unified School District has invested in PLCs through devoting time and training, based on the
teachings of Solution Tree. In turn, each administrator is working with a site level Guiding Coalition to guide the staff through
the PLC process. Elementary teachers meet with grade level district teams on a regular basis to identify enduring standards
and create common formative assessments. The time spent with the district PLC teams helps endure a common, viable
curriculum.
Stone Creek has revisted our mission, vision, and collective commitments. These commitments and beliefs drive every
decision to be focused on student learning. Grade level PLCs use common formative assessments, NWEA assessments, and
state assessments to measure student growth. Through disaggregated data, teachers analyze students' strength and growth
areas. The growth areas are revisited and concepts are retaught. The Stone Creek staff has also spent time visiting other
teachers' classrooms and sharing best practices.
Stone Creek teachers are trained to use a walk to intervention model for reading intervention. All students are given a universal
screener in reading, and with training from the reading specialist, teachers create platooning groups to meet the needs of all
students. Further reading professional development has been provided to all TK - 2nd grade teachers, SPED, and the reading
specialist through Orton Gillingham Training. A four-day training has taught teachers phonics and decoding strategies to
support all levels of readers.
