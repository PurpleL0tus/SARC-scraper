I am thrilled and privileged to be your principal at Park View Elementary. It is my sincere pleasure to work with
our dedicated staff and community to help provide an excellent educational experience for our students. At
Park View, we are committed to ensuring that all children receive the best support to learn and grow, and

Statewide Assessments
Statewide assessments (i.e., California Assessment of Student Performance and Progress [CAASPP] System
includes the Smarter Balanced Summative Assessments for students in the general education population and
the California Alternate Assessments [CAAs] for English language arts/literacy [ELA] and mathematics given
in grades three through eight and grade eleven. Only eligible students may participate in the administration
of the CAAs. CAAs items are aligned with alternate achievement standards, which are linked with the Com-
mon Core State Standards [CCSS] for students with the most significant cognitive disabilities).
The CAASPP System encompasses the following assessments and student participation requirements:
1. Smarter Balanced Summative Assessments and CAAs for ELA in grades three through eight and
grade eleven.
2. Smarter Balanced Summative Assessments and CAAs for mathematics in grades three through
eight and grade eleven.
3. California Science Test (CAST) and CAAs for Science in grades five, eight, and once in high school
(i.e., grade 10, 11 or 12).

School Facilities affect school safety.
Park View Elementary School provides a safe and clean environment for students, staff and volunteers. The
school is situated on 10 acres and opened its doors to staff and students in August 2022. This spacious build-
ing includes a library, music room, food-service area, multipurpose room, 36 permanent classrooms and two
playground areas, all of which are up-to-date and provide adequate space for students and staff. The large
grass field allows ample space to play, evacuate if necessary and host community events. The overall school
site is in excellent condition with the support of two full-time and two part-time custodians. When/if repairs
are needed, we look to see if the concern is under warranty or needs to be outsourced through our lead
maintenance operator.
Safety: The safety of students and staff is a primary concern of Park View Elementary School. All entrance
doors and gates are secured throughout the day requiring the use of an ID badge for access. The school is a
closed campus; all visitors must sign in at the office and wear identification badges while on school grounds
using Raptor Technologies. During recess, lunch, and before and after school, adult supervision is provided
by administration, teachers, and classified staff to ensure the safety of all individuals throughout the school
day.
Cleaning Process: A team of one full-time maintenance lead, one full-time custodian and two part-time cus-
todians ensure classrooms, restrooms and campus grounds are kept clean and safe. Park View visitors often
acknowledge how clean and maintained the campus appears.
Monthly building inspections are administered by the Mountain View School District to ensure all classrooms
and facilities are well maintained and provide a suitable learning environment.
About the School
Park View Elementary is home to 832 students who reside within the school site boundary. The student body
demographic profile, ethnic diversity, and multicultural population are reflective of the district and the city in
which it resides. This rich diversity is a great asset facing the multicultural world of the twenty-first century.
Some home languages that represent Park View are Mandarin, Spanish, Cantonese, Korean and English to
name a few. Our active English learner (EL) program allows for students and families to become involved and
partner in learning and strengthening English skills while on campus.
