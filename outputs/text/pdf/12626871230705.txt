General Information about the School Accountability Report Card (SARC)
SARC Overview By February 1 of each year, every school in California is required by state law to
publish a School Accountability Report Card (SARC). The SARC contains
information about the condition and performance of each California public school.
Under the Local Control Funding Formula (LCFF) all local educational agencies
(LEAs) are required to prepare a Local Control and Accountability Plan (LCAP),
which describes how they intend to meet annual school-specific goals for all pupils,
with specific activities to address state and local priorities. Additionally, data
reported in an LCAP is to be consistent with data reported in the SARC.
- For more information about SARC requirements and access to prior year
reports, see the California Department of Education (CDE) SARC web page at
https://www.cde.ca.gov/ta/ac/sa/.
- For more information about the LCFF or the LCAP, see the CDE LCFF web
page at https://www.cde.ca.gov/fg/aa/lc/.
- For additional information about the school, parents/guardians and community
members should contact the school principal or the district office.
DataQuest DataQuest is an online data tool located on the CDE DataQuest web page at
https://dq.cde.ca.gov/dataquest/ that contains additional information about this
school and comparisons of the school to the district and the county. Specifically,
DataQuest is a dynamic system that provides reports for accountability (e.g., test
data, enrollment, high school graduates, dropouts, course enrollments, staffing,
and data regarding English learners).
California School Dashboard The California School Dashboard (Dashboard)
https://www.caschooldashboard.org/ reflects California’s new accountability and
continuous improvement system and provides information about how LEAs and
schools are meeting the needs of California’s diverse student population. The
Dashboard contains reports that display the performance of LEAs, schools, and
student groups on a set of state and local measures to assist in identifying
strengths, challenges, and areas in need of improvement.
Internet Access Internet access is available at public libraries and other locations that are publicly
accessible (e.g., the California State Library). Access to the Internet at libraries and
public locations is generally provided on a first-come, first-served basis. Other use
restrictions may include the hours of operation, the length of time that a workstation
may be used (depending on availability), the types of software programs available
on a workstation, and the ability to print documents.

2023-24 School Description and Mission Statement
2023-24 School Description and Mission Statement
Principal's Comment
Arcata High School has a rich tradition of providing students with academic rigor, a welcoming and inclusive culture, and extra-
curricular opportunities. AHS features excellent instructors in all subject areas to serve students of all abilities. AHS features
programs that serve all students in the classroom and a myriad of extra-curricular opportunities and programs to engage all
students. I proudly represent an outstanding group of dedicated educators and support personnel. Arcata High School is
committed to excellence in education for all students in a caring and supportive environment that provides physical,
psychological, and emotional safety. AHS fosters lifelong learning, offering students access to outstanding academic, career
tech, and fine arts course options. An effective educational program is based upon a core curriculum that is anchored in high
standards and expectations for all, with special attention given to problem-solving and critical thinking. Arcata High School is
committed to serving all students and teaching values that will carry them to success in the future.

School Facility Conditions and Planned Improvements
School Facility Conditions and Planned Improvements
The facilities at Arcata High School are good and represent the pride of the Northern Humboldt Union High School District in
providing students and teachers with clean and modernized classrooms. The custodial staff maintains the school in a manner
that reflects the pride and sense of accomplishment that all members of the school community feel. Safety inspections are
conducted regularly, and any safety-related problems are addressed immediately, ensuring that every possible effort is made to
provide a safe and appropriate educational environment.
As a result of a bond measure, Arcata High School facilities have undergone remodels and upgrades that provide a truly
comfortable and productive learning environment. All classrooms are modern, clean, and well-lit. Recently, a new
track/soccer/football field & baseball field were completed to allow students more options during Physical Education class and
after-school athletics.
Facility grants have been secured to update our woodshop area, culinary room, agriculture outdoor space, sports facilities, and
the Fine Arts building.

2022-23 Career Technical Education Programs
2022-23 Career Technical Education Programs
Arcata High School offers a variety of Career Technical courses. Wood Shop, Metal Shop, Automotive Technology, Technology
Essentials, AP Computer Programming, Gaming, Video Production, Makers Workshop, Beginning/Intermediate/Advanced
Foods, Work Experience, and Personal Finance are courses that are offered. Students have the opportunity to take classes
beginning their freshman year and continuing through their senior year. The district is fortunate to have dedicated teachers
and community members who support the many programs offered. We continue to look at options for the future, especially in
technology, as this is a skill in our twenty-first-century economy where employment possibilities exist in all areas of career
technical education programs.
The Arcata Arts Institute (AAI) has blossomed into a model program that promotes art education and infusion throughout the
school and community. AAI works with local artists to bring professionals into the classroom to teach and mentor the art
students. Art showings are presented in local businesses every month in conjunction with Arcata Arts Alive. The Arcata Arts
Institute is dedicated to the idea that art matters. The AHS Agriculture program continues to grow with interest and academic
offerings. Through a statewide grant, the Agriculture program is offering additional AG Science courses and providing
supplemental educational materials to the program. This year, we have added Ag Mechanics & Ag Leadership to increase the
offerings in this program.
The Career & College Center (C&CC), Transition Partnership Program, and Career Pathways program are collaborating to
provide support for students as they transition from high school to college or directly into the workforce. The C&CC is a shining
example of the district’s commitment to helping students succeed. They regularly offer counseling for financial aid and college
applications, career Speaker Panels, Job Readiness Workshops, Career Exploration field trips, and individual counseling to
help students navigate postsecondary options.

2023-24 Opportunities for Parental Involvement
2023-24 Opportunities for Parental Involvement
Arcata High School has a strong tradition of parental involvement in curricular and extra-curricular activities. Arcata High
encourages all parents to remain involved in their students' educational lives. Parent participation in site council is robust.
Parent involvement in other opportunities includes Tigers Inc. (Parent Booster Club), School Site Council, Madrigal Parent
Group (Music Booster Group), ArMack Parent Group (Music Booster Group), The Arcata Arts Institute, and AHS Friends of
Agriculture Organization, as well as outside groups, like Safe and Sober Graduation Committee.
At Arcata High, many parents are actively involved as volunteers for athletic and extracurricular programs. Parents provide
support in many areas, from running a snack bar to mentoring students in the arts and academics.
AHS utilizes our Site Council and holds annual Educational Partners meeting(s) to gather feedback from
parents/students/faculty and community partners. Parents can inquire through the Principal’s Office for information about these
organizations. In addition, parents contribute to the Athletic Advisory Committee which advises the school board in updating the

2023-24 School Safety Plan
2023-24 School Safety Plan
Arcata High School takes pride in maintaining a safe physical and emotional environment for our students, staff, and the
extended community. Our site Safety Committee has a close working relationship with the Arcata Police Department, Arcata
Fire Department, First Responders, Humboldt County Public Health and the Humboldt Office of Education Risk Management
Team. Each semester we hold safety trainings such as earthquake, fire, and lockdown drills. Our comprehensive safety plan is
reviewed annually, sent to the School Board for approval, and posted on the AHS website.
The District Safety Committee holds regular safety meetings to improve and monitor each site's progress related to safety
procedures and protocols. The primary goal of the District and Site Safety Committees is developing and enhancing emergency
readiness plans at the school and implementing policies that promote good mental health.

Professional Development
Professional Development
NHUHSD continues to use Instructional Coaches to infuse professional development programs within the district. The Northern
Humboldt Union High School District has always had a long-standing commitment to supporting professional development
within our district. Over the past few years, the Arcata High School staff has had many opportunities to attend workshops and
training that promote improved instruction within their programs. AHS has worked to implement its MTSS, tier 1 supports by
creating a school tier 1 leadership team and providing training for staff in positive behavior supports and universal design for
learning. In addition to days devoted to PD, Instructional coaches and TOSA's provide instructional updates at selected staff

Professional Development
meetings. Professional development is also offered on an annual basis for teachers of Advanced Placement courses. Finally,
Department Chairs have the opportunity to submit requests for additional PD opportunities that are specific to their department.
Arcata High School continues to participate in the state-recognized New Teacher Induction Program (NTIP). This program
matches beginning teachers with experienced mentor teachers in order to promote a supportive environment for new teaching
professionals. Additionally, AHS has a 1.0 FTE Instructional Coach that works with our beginning teachers throughout their first
few years (at the school) and provides training and curricular support. The instructional coach offers three PLC’s at AHS.
There is a Level 1 and a Level 2 PLC for Universal design for learning. These groups are grant-funded, and participants
receive a $1000 stipend at the end of the year. There is also a voluntary PLC for teachers in the NCSOE Induction program
who are working toward clearing their credentials. This program has proven extremely beneficial in providing new employees
with the tools necessary to experience success as beginning teachers. We are committed to providing extensive support for
our teacher’s professional growth to maximize our effectiveness in providing an excellent educational experience for our
students.
