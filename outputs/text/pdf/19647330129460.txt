General Information about the School Accountability Report Card (SARC)
SARC Overview By February 1 of each year, every school in California is required by state law to
publish a School Accountability Report Card (SARC). The SARC contains
information about the condition and performance of each California public school.
Under the Local Control Funding Formula (LCFF) all local educational agencies
(LEAs) are required to prepare a Local Control and Accountability Plan (LCAP),
which describes how they intend to meet annual school-specific goals for all pupils,
with specific activities to address state and local priorities. Additionally, data
reported in an LCAP is to be consistent with data reported in the SARC.
- For more information about SARC requirements and access to prior year
reports, see the California Department of Education (CDE) SARC web page at
https://www.cde.ca.gov/ta/ac/sa/.
- For more information about the LCFF or the LCAP, see the CDE LCFF web
page at https://www.cde.ca.gov/fg/aa/lc/.
- For additional information about the school, parents/guardians and community
members should contact the school principal or the district office.
DataQuest DataQuest is an online data tool located on the CDE DataQuest web page at
https://dq.cde.ca.gov/dataquest/ that contains additional information about this
school and comparisons of the school to the district and the county. Specifically,
DataQuest is a dynamic system that provides reports for accountability (e.g., test
data, enrollment, high school graduates, dropouts, course enrollments, staffing,
and data regarding English learners).

2023-24 School Description and Mission Statement
2023-24 School Description and Mission Statement
KIPP Vida Prep (KVP) has a vision that is focused on developing students who are strong in intellect and character. We believe
in providing quality education and pushing for academic excellence from the beginning of a student’s educational career is
necessary for student success. Our academic program is standards-based (using California Common Core standards),
rigorous, and pushes toward student-directed learning that supports problem-solving and critical thinking in collaborative
settings. Teachers work together to design assessments and other engaging learning opportunities that will allow for on-going
analysis, synthesis and evaluation of skills and concepts that will inform instruction. Through academics, we aim to raise our
student’s intellectual ability and increase the college matriculation rate for students from South Los Angeles. We know that
academics alone will not drive our students to and through college, but that educating the whole child is what will lead students
to live choice-filled lives and ultimately have a positive impact on society.
About Our School
KIPP Vida Preparatory Academy is a public charter school with a staff that prides itself in being a community of learners
dedicated to our students. Through teamwork and data-driven instruction, we work to give our students a competitive edge to

in a state of the art building with a rich history. The building itself is over 100 years old but was completely remodeled and
brought up to modern code in 2017 (Fire, Electrical, Seismic Fitting, Technology and More).

1. Building out of a multi-million dollar state of the art recess and play area

3. Updated and renovated staff MPR and Lounge
4. Installation of brand new ceiling mounted projectors, appletvs, recessed sound w microphones.
5. Upgraded filtration systems
6. Security Cameras
7. Installation of a Community Garden
8. Upgraded outdoor lighting and installation of filtered potable water systems for students
9. Purchase of a professional stage and chairs for student performances
10. Purchase of a theater quality sound system for school performances.

2023-24 Opportunities for Parental Involvement
2023-24 Opportunities for Parental Involvement
KIPP Vida Preparatory Academy encourages parent volunteer participation in several activities and events throughout the year.
KIPP Vida Preparatory Academy believes having parents directly involved leads to a deep connection with both the success of
their students within the school and the lasting footprint the school produces within the community.
A few notable opportunities are:
1. "Coffee with Admin" monthly meetings to discuss and receive feedback from parents in areas such as student growth, social-
emotional support, teaching and learning, school highlights, COVID-19 updates, etc. Families are also welcome to attend in
person or virtually .
2."Vida Time" (when outdoors) , a weekly whole school assembly where the entire school community comes together to
discuss a wide variety of topics: bullying, school safety, school values and how to embody them, current events, and school
campaigns.
3. Culture month recognitions with performances that parents are also encouraged to attend, (LatinX Heritage, AAPI, Black
History Month, and more)
4. Electives Team Showcases: At different points throughout the the year, the electives team (Dance, Theater, Spanish, Art,
P.E) puts on showcases to demonstrate what leaders are learning.
5. Family Leadership Academy: A council that consists of the school's leaders, staff and families in order to come to together to
learn how to best advocate for our school community.
Lastly, using a digital app-based platform called ParentSquare, we are able to stay in regular communication regarding school
and community happenings. Our weekly family newsletter helps us communicate week-to-week announcements, reminders,
and updates.
For more information related to these and other parental involvement opportunities, please contact our Family Engagement
Lead, Diana Corado, DCorado@kippsocal.org.

2023-24 School Safety Plan
2023-24 School Safety Plan
The safety and security of all KIPP Vida Preparatory Academy students and staff is our highest priority. All staff members
receive training in a variety of emergency scenarios and use practice drills conducted every month to develop and grow our
preparedness. The current safety plan was created in August 2023 and finalized in October 2023 and continues to be
discussed with staff following each emergency drill. Members of KIPP Vida Preparatory Academy's staff receive general
emergency preparedness training before school starts /during summer presentations, and prior to each monthly drill. These
trainings include key elements of the plan and proper steps to carry it out. This comprehensive plan is also sent to all LAPD

Fiscal Year 2022-23 Types of Services Funded
Our instructional program provides core subject and enrichment activities for 562 students, grades TK to 4th during the 2023-
24 school year. Core subjects include literacy, math, science, and social studies. Our enrichment program includes visual arts,
Spanish, theatre, dance and physical education.
KIPP Vida aims to not only provide every student a quality education, but also the experience and tools to cultivate active
leaders, with strong self-identity and prepare them to thrive in college and life. We see every student as ours and live by the
belief that we teach in schools not classrooms. We share responsibility for our children by building strong relationships with our
families and the surrounding community. We believe actions speak louder than words. We strive to live the change we want to
see, which is reflected in our core curriculum, enrichment program, and all family engagement events throughout the year.
We are committed to working with families to provide the targeted strategies and resources that will best help families to
support their children at home to reach their Big Goals. We are continuously evaluating the needs of our families to develop
workshops and resources to help create environments for our students to succeed in and out of the classroom.
KVP offers an ASES-funded afterschool program to assist and support our students. The goal of our program is to continue the
support students are receiving during the regular school day in a safe constructive environment during after-school hours.

Professional Development
Professional Development
Professional Development is top priority for all KIPP Vida Prep staff members. We believe that when our teachers grow, our
students grow. We are committed to developing the instructional practice of all of our educators. The professional development
schedule begins during the 2-3 weeks leading up to the start of the new school year and continues during workshops held
every Monday afternoon for 1-2 hours and several professional development days scheduled throughout the year. Priorities for
professional development are determined based on priorities set forth by the School Leader and leadership team, and ongoing
teacher feedback and input. All planned PD topics are revisited multiple times a year to ensure all areas addressed are relevant
to the needs of the staff and the students. Teachers are also supported through in-class observations, teacher-administrator
meetings, and student performance data reporting.
