General Information about the School Accountability Report Card (SARC)
SARC Overview By February 1 of each year, every school in California is required by state law to
publish a School Accountability Report Card (SARC). The SARC contains
information about the condition and performance of each California public school.
Under the Local Control Funding Formula (LCFF) all local educational agencies
(LEAs) are required to prepare a Local Control and Accountability Plan (LCAP),
which describes how they intend to meet annual school-specific goals for all pupils,
with specific activities to address state and local priorities. Additionally, data
reported in an LCAP is to be consistent with data reported in the SARC.
- For more information about SARC requirements and access to prior year
reports, see the California Department of Education (CDE) SARC web page at
https://www.cde.ca.gov/ta/ac/sa/.
- For more information about the LCFF or the LCAP, see the CDE LCFF web
page at https://www.cde.ca.gov/fg/aa/lc/.
- For additional information about the school, parents/guardians and community
members should contact the school principal or the district office.
DataQuest DataQuest is an online data tool located on the CDE DataQuest web page at
https://dq.cde.ca.gov/dataquest/ that contains additional information about this
school and comparisons of the school to the district and the county. Specifically,
DataQuest is a dynamic system that provides reports for accountability (e.g., test
data, enrollment, high school graduates, dropouts, course enrollments, staffing,
and data regarding English learners).
California School Dashboard The California School Dashboard (Dashboard)
https://www.caschooldashboard.org/ reflects California’s new accountability and
continuous improvement system and provides information about how LEAs and
schools are meeting the needs of California’s diverse student population. The
Dashboard contains reports that display the performance of LEAs, schools, and
student groups on a set of state and local measures to assist in identifying
strengths, challenges, and areas in need of improvement.
Internet Access Internet access is available at public libraries and other locations that are publicly
accessible (e.g., the California State Library). Access to the Internet at libraries and
public locations is generally provided on a first-come, first-served basis. Other use
restrictions may include the hours of operation, the length of time that a workstation
may be used (depending on availability), the types of software programs available
on a workstation, and the ability to print documents.

2023-24 School Description and Mission Statement
2023-24 School Description and Mission Statement
District Mission- The mission of the Stanislaus Union School District as the premier district of choice in partnership with the
community is to ensure the ultimate educational experience for all children.
The District's goals are: 1) Enhance practices and programs with a focus on instruction of effective State Standards for quality
learning by all students; 2) Continue to prioritize writing and provide consistent expectations aligned with state standards at all
school sites; 3) Provide and enhance site support and develop community/business partnerships for Visual and Performing
Arts; 4) Continue to optimize across all curricular domains, the use of technology resources for students, teachers and school
sites as written in the District Technology Plan; 5) Increase community/school engagement through technology as well as
extend technology for communication and education; 6) Update and promote wellness curriculum relevant to the whole child,
i.e. nutrition, child development, bullying, social and peer interactions, substance abuse prevention, and family support
services.
School Mission: At Agnes Baptist, we ensure that all students learn at high levels.
School Vision: The Agnes Baptist Elementary School educational community creates a challenging learning environment that
establishes high expectations for academic success, providing opportunities for enrichment, intervention, and remediation. In
addition, we develop the whole child - academically, physically, socially, and emotionally. Our educational community confronts
and embraces challenges with confidence and resilience, while fostering a lifelong passion for learning. Our school is
recognized and respected in the community for collaboration, innovation, and academic growth. We believe that all children can
and will learn if given enough time and targeted support. As such, our vision is to continue to develop and perfect our
Response to Intervention System of supports for all of our students through the work our teachers and support staff are doing
as a Professional Learning Community.
School Description: The information included in this School Accountability Report Card for Agnes Baptist School is based on
data from the 2022-2023 school year. Agnes Baptist Elementary School houses Transitional Kindergarten through Sixth (6th)
Grade. It is a mixed socio-economic, suburban school. Agnes Baptist Elementary School offers a variety of programs, such as
Gifted and Talented Education (GATE), Special Education, After School Programs and Title I programs to address specific
needs of all students. The parent community is actively involved in the education process.

School Facility Conditions and Planned Improvements
School Facility Conditions and Planned Improvements
Agnes Baptist Elementary School believes that to build a positive school culture the school site must be safe, clean, and
functional. The site receives feedback from community members through its website, surveys, personal contact, SSC, ELAC,
and parents club meetings in regard to the condition and needs of the site. The information gathered is to ensure that the site is
well maintained, clean, safe, and in proper working order.
The District takes great efforts to ensure that all schools are clean, safe, and functional. To assist in this effort, the District uses
a facility survey instrument developed by the State of California Office of Public School construction. The results of this survey
will be available at the school office, at the District Office, or on the internet at http://www.opsc.dgs.ca.gov/. Below is more
specific information on the condition of the school and the efforts made to ensure that students are provided with a clean, safe
and functional learning environment.
The school opened in 1990. The school has 30 classrooms, a multipurpose room, a library and an administration building. The
main campus was built in 1990. Six portable classrooms were added in 2001 and two new portables were added in the fall of
2013. District maintenance staff ensures that the repairs necessary to keep the school in good repair and working order are
completed in a timely manner. A work order process is used to ensure efficient service and that emergency repairs are given
the highest priority. Providing a clean learning environment is a district priority. The principal works daily with the custodial staff
to develop cleaning schedules to ensure a clean and safe school.
The District participates in the State School Deferred Maintenance Program, which provides state matching funds on a dollar-
for-dollar basis, to assist school districts with expenditures for major repair or replacement of existing school building
components. Typically, this includes roofing, plumbing, heating, air conditioning, electrical systems, interior or exterior painting,
and floor systems. The District recently updated play structures on site.

2023-24 Opportunities for Parental Involvement
2023-24 Opportunities for Parental Involvement
Agnes Baptist Elementary School is committed to encouraging parental involvement. Numerous opportunities are available for
parents to get involved with our school community including: Back to School Night, Parent Teacher Conferences, Open House,
Annual Title I Parent Meeting, School Site Council, English Learner Advisory Committee, Parent Club events, grade level field
trips, Individualized Education Plan meetings, Student Study Team meetings, volunteering in the classroom, and family
centered activities (Read to Succeed, Family Reading Night, Book Fairs, Winter Store, All Star Store, Jog-a-thon, etc.). These
opportunities allow parents a direct voice in the educational opportunities and lend a voice in the implementation of the school
wide program. Further involvement is offered through the establishment and monitoring of the school mission and values. This
includes but is not limited to surveys, open forums, and informational meetings to allow input and feedback as the process
begins to set the climate and culture of Agnes Baptist Elementary.
The district sponsors further events such as the Science Fair, District Book Fair, and school board meetings. These events
provide parents the opportunity to share in the educational activities of their students. The district further offers parents the
opportunity to be involved in district committees, making the availability of the district bilingual liaison for district and site events,
providing translation services for all documentation sent home to families, providing blackboard connect messages to all
families concerning district and site information, and providing training to staff and others when the needs arise.
Agnes Baptist Elementary will continue to host family nights again. Agnes Baptist Elementary staff held a very successful
Family Art Night at the beginning of the year and have plans to hold a Family STEM night and a Family Reading night before
the end of the year.

2023-24 School Safety Plan
2023-24 School Safety Plan
Agnes Baptist Elementary School has an adopted safety plan that is reviewed and revised annually. The plan was developed
by a committee comprised of the administration as well as representatives from the teaching and classified staff. The plan was
further reviewed by a team of district administration to include common language and procedures within the district. There are
11 sections to the plan: (1) Introduction; (2) Current Status; (3) Child Abuse; (4) Disaster Procedures; (5) District Policy; (6)
Teacher Notification; (7) Sexual Harassment; (8) Dress Code; (9) Safe Ingress/Egress; (10) Safe and Orderly Environment;
and (11) Discipline. The most recent update to the Safety Plan was completed in October 2023. The final safety plan will be
approved by the SUSD school board in March 2023.
The site safety program provides regular monitoring and inspection of all facilities and grounds for safety by the District Risk
Manager. The site is continually implementing improvements for security in an effort to fully secure the campus. The has site
participated with the district on a joint task force concerning emergency procedures and protocols (active shooter scenario) and
has the protocols and procedures concerning this possible emergency crisis. For example, the staff was trained on safety
protocols when there is a dangerous intruder on campus. This training is called, "Run, Hide, Fight." Agnes Baptist Elementary
School continues to review the safety plan with the committee and district administration to better align procedures in an on-
going process.

2022-23 Types of Services Funded
Fiscal Year 2022-23 Types of Services Funded
The General Fund per pupil expenditure for pupils in the Stanislaus Union School District was used to determine programs and
services to be provided in the 2023-2024 school year. Agnes Baptist Elementary School also received School Based
Coordinated Program (SBCP) funds to provide additional services to all pupils in grades K-6. These funds provided
supplemental services for support personnel, e.g. supplemental materials and an instructional aide, staff development, PE
equipment, supplies, technology, conferences, PBIS implementation, and SES (After School Program Intervention). In
addition, the school received Title I funds which provided supplemental reading and math services for designated pupils
through response to intervention for all grade levels, staff development, additional support from a Board Certified Behavior
Analyst, an on-site therapist and Student Assistant Specialist, and to purchase equipment and materials. Supplemental
services (additional personnel materials and equipment) to assist English Proficient pupils acquire English.

Professional Development
Professional Development
All teachers and Paraprofessionals have met the NCLB requirements as a highly qualified staff. The district is providing
support through instructional coaches, GATE Identification, district grade level meetings once per month, visual learning, and
utilization of components of the CAASPP (State Testing) on interim assessment usage and training.
Classroom teachers and other school personnel participate in a variety of staff development activities. The activities included
professional development days under the guidance of the district's Assistant Superintendent of Curriculum & Instruction.
The staff focused on adopted curriculum resources, such as Benchmark ELA and Eureka Math programs. In addition, the staff
in participating in on-going Professional Learning Communities and Response to Intervention practices.
To support these endeavors, the site has on-going professional coaching to support the school and grade-level teams in
implementation through the use of educational consultants.
To target specific needs of student populations, staff has also participated in GLAD strategies to support English Language
Learners, Pro-Act to support Special Education programs, Depth and Complexity professional development for GATE
instruction and Universal Pre-Kindergarten support for transitional kindergarten and kindergarten staff.
